## HIGH

### 1. R-36 (and C-5): claims that are not yet true or not measured
- Where: `index.html` lines 55 ("Usually a few seconds"), 155 ("see the result in seconds"), 109 ("the next one answers"), 111 ("The server does not store it"), 149 (FAQ, same claim), 147 ("Free tries are not saved"), 161 (footer).
- Why: the server that would make these true does not exist. Speed has never been measured. The page states plans as facts.
- Fix: remove the time claims. Reword unbuilt behaviour as intent ("will", "designed to") or add a visible "In development" line. Put the claims back once the server is verified.
- Weight: high for honesty. The demo bar shows "Demo mode" during review, but a released page would not.

### 2. R-38 (and C-5, "Demo Without a Product"): asks for email with no legal pages and no live product
- Where: `login.html` and the "Sign in" links. `static/` has no terms or privacy page, and no page mentions them.
- Why: the skill says a page that asks for sign-up needs Terms of Use and a Privacy Policy, and must show the product working or say honestly that it is not shipped.
- Fix: the owner supplies the real terms and privacy text (I will not invent legal text), then add the pages and footer links. Until then, show "In development" and do not collect emails.
- Weight: high before any public launch; none while it is a design review.

### 3. R-23: logo and favicon created without confirmation
- Where: the brand mark in every header (`class="brand"`, line 17 in `index.html`) and `favicon.svg`: a yellow square with a prompt glyph.
- Why: the skill requires asking before creating a logo, or using the product name as text or a `[LOGO]` placeholder.
- Fix: replace with the name "Prompt Code" in text only, until the owner approves a mark. The name may also change before release.
- Weight: small to fix; it also touches every page header.

### 4. R-25 and R-34: placeholder text colour is left to the browser
- Where: `css/style.css` has no `::placeholder` rule. The idea boxes and the email field use placeholders.
- Evidence (skill checker): a common browser default (#757575) is 4.61:1 on white, which passes narrowly, but 3.76:1 on the dark panel, which fails. A lighter default (#A9A9A9) is 2.35:1 on white and fails.
- Fix: set the placeholder colour to the muted text colour in both themes (7.6 to 8.3:1 measured).
- Weight: real and easy. One rule.

### 5. R-32 and R-34: focus ring invisible in the demo bar
- Where: the Scenario menu in `.demo-bar` (demo mode only).
- Evidence: ink focus ring on the ink bar is 1.00:1 in the light theme; yellow on the pale bar is 1.25:1 in the dark theme.
- Fix: give the demo bar its own focus colour that contrasts with its background.
- Weight: low in practice (the bar is removed for release), but it is a keyboard trap for reviewers.

### 6. R-27: no visible loading state on lists
- Where: History and Models show a blank area while their data loads. The converter keeps its "will appear here" message while it converts; only the button label changes.
- Fix: a visible "Loading history..." and "Loading models..." line, and a "Converting..." line in the result area, each announced politely.
- Weight: small to fix; noticeable on slow connections.

### 7. R-03 (and the mobile layout skill): phone layout is not a designed state (by code inspection)
- Where: `css/style.css`.
  - Section and hero padding stay at 72px on phones (`.section`, `.hero`); the sign-in card keeps a 64px top margin.
  - The header is a wrapping row: seven items on the landing page, with no menu or compact pattern.
  - `.frame` uses `min-height:100vh`, which counts the browser toolbar on phones.
  - The card grid has a 260px minimum, which overflows on screens narrower than about 290px.
- Fix: a smaller spacing step under about 640px; a compact header (one scrolling row or a labelled "Menu"); `100dvh` with a fallback; `minmax(min(100%, 260px), 1fr)`.
- Weight: medium. Real phones are likely to look loose, not broken.

### 8. R-35: not verified in a real browser
- Where: the whole UI.
- Evidence: 26 simulated-DOM tests pass. Nothing has been rendered, so layout, both themes, phone width, 200% zoom, the on-screen keyboard, the real clipboard and Escape on the real dialog are unchecked.
- Fix: open each page at phone, tablet and desktop width in light and dark, click every control, and record the list (the skill asks for a recorded click-through). I cannot render pages in this environment.
- Weight: high for confidence. Several other findings could change after this check.

## MEDIUM

### 9. R-01 and R-29: five pastel chip colours with no meaning
- Where: the example chips on the landing page (42 and four more), plus the lilac recommendation box and lemon selected row.
- Why: the colours are not tied to anything (class design is not "mint"). The palette becomes yellow plus five pastels plus three status tints, which the skill's "pastel blocks" and "too many colours" tells describe.
- Fix: one chip style (outlined, neutral) and keep yellow as the single accent.
- Weight: medium. Also removes the border problem in 13.

### 10. R-14: identical cards in three sections
- Where: examples (79), reliability, and resources: each is three equal cells with the same padding.
- Fix: vary by importance: examples as one wide before-and-after, reliability as a short plain list, resources as a plain list.

### 11. R-06: the headline font changes with the device
- Where: `--display` is a system serif stack (Iowan Old Style, Palatino Linotype, Palatino, Georgia). Different devices show different faces, and no reason for the choice is written down.
- Fix: write the reason, or self-host one openly licensed display font (this also keeps visitor addresses away from font servers). Owner decision, since it also needs a licence check.

### 12. R-08: arrow on the Convert buttons
- Where: Convert on the landing page and the converter. The purpose is not written down.
- Fix: write the purpose (direction from input to result) or remove the arrow. Low impact.

### 13. Non-text contrast (human skill, WCAG 1.4.11): chips have no visible boundary
- Evidence: chip fills against the page are 1.13, 1.27, 1.20, 1.17 and 1.04:1 (target 3:1). Their text passes (6.1 to 8.2:1 against dark text), so the label identifies each chip, but the skill asks for 3:1 on boundaries.
- The primary yellow button is 1.31:1 against the page but has a 1px ink border, so it passes.
- Fix: a 1px ink border on chips, or the outlined style from 9. No R-number; treated as MEDIUM.

### 14. Part 3 and R-37: no Design Read, no dials, no DESIGN.md
- Where: the project. Direction exists only in `LANDING-PAGE.md` section 4.1 and in the conversation.
- Fix: write a short `DESIGN.md` with identity, palette, type, and the three dials. My reading of the build as it stands: ENERGY 2, RHYTHM 1, MOTION 1. The owner confirms or changes these. Part 3 has no tier; treated as MEDIUM.

## LOW

### 16. R-15 and C-3: the closing call to action goes nowhere new
- Where: "Write your first prompt" ends with a button labelled "Back to the top" (line 156).
- Fix: replace with a button that focuses the idea box ("Start with an example"), or remove the section.

### 17. R-21: no manual light and dark choice

- Fix: add a toggle, or record why following the system is enough. Optional.

### 18. R-31: reasons not written down

### 19. C-3: internal note shown to visitors
